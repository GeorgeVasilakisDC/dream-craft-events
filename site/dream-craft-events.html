Who we are

We believe every event
deserves to be extraordinary

Dream Craft Events is a boutique event planning studio based in Heraklion, Crete. We specialise in weddings, baptisms, and private celebrations — crafting each event with care, elegance, and an eye for every detail that makes your moment truly unique.

Weddings

Your most important day, planned to perfection. From intimate ceremonies to grand celebrations, we handle every detail so you simply live the moment.

02

Baptisms

A beautiful milestone, celebrated with grace. We create a warm, elegant atmosphere that honours this special occasion for your family and guests.

Private Events

Birthdays, engagements, anniversaries — every personal celebration deserves its own unique touch and impeccable execution.

Corporate Events

Presentations, gala dinners, team gatherings — we bring the same elegance and precision to your professional events.
